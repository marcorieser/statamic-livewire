Named, always, lazy (with a placeholder), and looped islands with captured scope — each updates independently without re-rendering the rest of the component. The addon's livewire:island tag piggybacks Livewire's own island pipeline (Blade's @island/@endisland directives), so most of this behaves identically either way — except the looped island, see the Blade section below.

Blade

The looped, captured-scope island is missing here on purpose — Blade's native island directive can't do it (its with: expression is re-evaluated in the island's own isolated compiled file, with no access to the enclosing loop's variable). Capturing the surrounding scope for looped islands is one of the things this addon adds over stock Blade.
